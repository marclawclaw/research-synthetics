# Alchemix (alUSD, alETH and V3 alAssets)

Status: live. Users deposit yield-bearing collateral and borrow synthetic "alAssets" (alUSD tracking USD, alETH tracking ETH) that are repaid automatically from the collateral's yield. The synthetic is therefore a CDP debt token whose peg is held by a redemption queue (the Transmuter) rather than by auction liquidation of positions. V2 (2021 to 2026) was marketed as non-liquidating; V3, live since April 2026, raised the loan-to-value to 90% and reintroduced liquidations.

## How it works

### User perspective

Deposit collateral (V2: ETH, DAI, USDC, USDT, FRAX and yield-bearing wrappers) and borrow up to 50% of its value as the matching alAsset (V2). The yield on the collateral automatically repays the debt over time; the user may self-liquidate (repay from collateral) at any time with no lock-in. V3: borrow up to 90% LTV against MYT collateral, a Morpho Vault2-based aggregated yield token. V2 debt is denominated in the same currency as the collateral, so price moves do not threaten it; V3 can liquidate positions if the MYT loses value or LTV exceeds the liquidation threshold.

### Protocol perspective

- **Alchemist:** holds collateral, tracks debt, enforces `minimumCollateralization` and a `collateralizationLowerBound` (liquidation LTV is `1 / collateralizationLowerBound`). Default values are not in the contract docs retrieved: [NOT FOUND].
- **One Alchemist per alAsset (docs, V2):** "There is one Alchemist for each alAsset on each chain." "Alchemists can have multiple yield strategies but can only issue one type of alAsset." The alUSD Alchemist "issues alUSD but can accept yield-bearing tokens for any approved stablecoin"; "alETH can be borrowed against ETH-denominated yield token deposits." So each Alchemist issues one synth ([[rfp/appendix/cdp-isolation]], "Alchemix: one Alchemist per alAsset"). Source: [Alchemix user docs, llms-full.txt](https://alchemix-finance.gitbook.io/user-docs/llms-full.txt) (271,365 characters; all four quotes found verbatim on 2026-11-17). The current [Alchemist page](https://alchemix-finance.gitbook.io/user-docs/alchemix-ecosystem/alchemist.md) words the first point as "Each alAsset on each chain is managed by a dedicated Alchemist" (read 2026-11-17); the "one Alchemist for each alAsset" sentence is in llms-full.txt but not on that page. V3 not checked; separate Transmuters and per-Alchemist debt accounting are not addressed in the part read.
- **Transmuter (peg mechanism):** alAsset holders stake into the Transmuter and receive the underlying 1:1 as yield is released to it; in V3, debt is "earmarked" as Transmuter deposits vest and the earmarked part of each position's collateral is swapped to repay it.
- **Peg:** a soft peg: redemption at 1:1 via the queue is slow, so the alAsset can trade below the underlying while the queue is long (alETH traded below its 0.991 ETH soft target after the 2023 Curve incident; Alchemix Q4 2023 report, secondary snippet).

## Key behaviours

- [[patterns/synthetic-collateral-models]] : yield-bearing collateral CDP with queue redemption.
- [[patterns/cdp-synthetics-landscape]] : classed as a per-position CDP; the debt is repaid by yield not by user cash flow.
- [[patterns/oracle-pricing-model]] : V2 depends on the vaulted collateral's value for each yield token (liquidations and withdrawals are blocked if a yield token drops sharply below its expected value).

## Architecture decisions

- **Same-asset borrowing (V2):** alUSD against USD-denominated collateral, alETH against ETH-denominated, so no price-driven liquidation. Cost: only 50% LTV (200% collateralisation).
- **V3 rebuild:** MYT vault, Alchemist, Transmuter; positions migrated by the multisig from a snapshot. The DAO treasury covered conversion slippage up to 0.25%. Source: [Alchemix V3 migration docs](https://alchemix-v3-docs.vercel.app/user/v3-migration).
- **Per-chain parameters:** the Early Transmutation Fee and the Borrower Redemption Fee differ by chain (Base 0.10%, Arbitrum 2.50% early exit).

## Differentiators

- Self-repaying loans: no interest and no required repayment schedule.
- V2 had no third-party liquidation of positions (V2 FAQ).
- Capital efficiency in V3 (90% LTV) comes from the MYT collateral and "temporal leverage" (earmarked collateral continues to earn until redeemed).

## Limitations and criticisms

- TVL fell from $889.5M (December 2021) to $32.75M for V3 (2026-11-16): the model depends on collateral yield, and borrowers get a loan that repays slowly (V2 FAQ example: a 50% LTV loan at 10% APR takes about 5 years).
- Vault strategy risk: if the MYT or a yield strategy loses funds, V3 liquidates positions above the liquidation LTV and Transmuter users may receive partial redemptions until bad debt clears.
- V3 audit history: Immunefi audit competition reports dated October and November 2025 list critical and high findings on the Transmuter and Alchemist contracts (submitted reports; fix status not checked) per [Immunefi reports](https://reports.immunefi.com/alchemix-v3/58163-sc-critical-total-loss-of-user-funds-in-claim-redemption) (search result only).
- Incidents: June 2021 alETH vault exploit of about $6.5M from a wrong index in debt calculations (DefiLlama hacks list labels it "Alchemix V2", but its first TVL date is 2021-11-18, so the label is likely wrong); 2023 Curve exploit of the alETH/ETH pool, after which the exploiter returned funds but arbitrageurs caused a permanent loss of 3,856 alETH to LPs (Alchemix Q4 2023 report via search snippet).
