# CDP on LEZ: launch collateral evidence

Evidence for the collateral set of the synth CDP on LEZ (sXMR, sBTC and others). The working position under discussion backs one synth token with several collaterals, starting at mainnet launch with wrapped ETH from RFP-022 and wrapped BTC from the planned Strata (Alpen Labs) bridge, with native Logos collateral (the gas token and the reflexive stablecoin (RFP-013)) added later or allowed from day one under a cap on its share of total collateral. This note holds only the facts behind that position: what the Logos specifications say, what is public about Strata, which protocols cap one collateral's share and how, and what happened when bridged collateral failed. It makes no recommendation. Part map: [[rfp/appendix/cdp-notion-map]]. Written 2026-11-19 (agent-assisted); not yet fact-checked.

Terms: an **absolute cap** is a limit in units or USD (a debt ceiling, a supply cap); a **relative cap** is a limit on one collateral's share of the total backing.

### 1.3 RFP-019: TWAP needs a pool with liquidity

All lines from `RFPs/RFP-019-twap-oracle.md` at `920fad9`; `status: closed`, delivered internally (`tmp/budget-compare/comparison.md` line 20).

- **Native assets have only the DEX.** "LGS (the Logos token) and the reflexive stablecoin trade on a LEZ DEX (RFP-004) and nowhere else at launch. No off-chain publisher (Chainlink, Pyth, RedStone) has data to sign for these pairs ... The only honest source is the LEZ DEX itself, read through an on-chain TWAP" (lines 69 to 75).
- **Thin pools are cheap to move.** "with thin liquidity, a PoS validator controlling two consecutive blocks can manipulate the TWAP accumulator at a cost roughly equal to the round-trip swap fees and price impact"; "The attack cost scales linearly with pool depth, so pools with $1M in liquidity offer far less protection than pools with $100M" (lines 102 to 107). Manipulating "a thin LEZ DEX pool" is "cheap at chain launch" (lines 233 to 234).
- **What RFP-019 requires.** Per-block tick-delta truncation, sampling at block boundaries, and a documented minimum observation window with a manipulation-cost analysis at $1M, $10M, $50M and $100M pool depth (lines 107 to 111, 392 to 400).
- **Wrapped assets.** Wrapped BTC and ETH are "priced primarily by external oracles" and "also benefit from an on-chain TWAP cross-check where a LEZ DEX pair exists with sufficient liquidity" (lines 86 to 93).
- **Composition is out of scope.** Chaining feeds such as `LGS/USD = LGS/wBTC × wBTC/USD` is not specified or implemented by RFP-019 (lines 439 to 443).
- **Testnet.** An AMM, a TWAP oracle program and the token program are deployed on LEZ testnet v0.2, and the docs walk through creating a pool and publishing a TWAP price (`logos-docs-main` at `75e541c2`, `docs/lez/programs/trade-tokens-on-lez-amm-program.md` lines 16 to 34, 66). This page is not in `logos-docs` at `03626bf2`.

### 1.4 RFP-013: the reflexive stablecoin floats

All lines from `RFPs/RFP-013-reflexive-stablecoin-protocol.md` at `920fad9`; `status: closed`.

- "Build a non-pegged, reflexive stablecoin protocol ... backed by a reference token collateral and uses an autonomous feedback controller to adjust a floating redemption price" (lines 55 to 58); "reflexive, non-pegged, and over-collateralized" (lines 87 to 88). The redemption price drifts with the computed rate (lines 107 to 110).
- Single reference collateral: "Multi-collateral positions" are out of scope (line 239).
- Its controller reads the stablecoin "priced in its reference collateral (a LEZ-native pair, e.g. against LGS or wBTC)", which only the LEZ DEX can price (RFP-019 lines 78 to 85, 433 to 438). The choice of reference collateral belongs to RFP-013 (RFP-019 line 436).
- Inference: a USD price for the reflexive stablecoin would need a DEX TWAP against its reference collateral times that collateral's USD feed, which is the composition RFP-019 leaves out of scope. No USD feed for it is specified anywhere found.
- Status of the code: the internal implementation (about 8.9k Rust lines in lez-programs, pinned to v0.2.5-rc2) "has no liquidation code, no mini-app and no private path" (`tmp/budget-compare/comparison.md` line 43).

## Conflicts and gaps

- **Token name.** "LGO" (logos-lips, logos-docs, roadmap) against "LGS" (RFP-019, RFP-020, the oracle slides).
- **External Logos token price at launch.** RFP-019 says none (lines 69 to 75); the oracle slides assume an external LGS/USD path (lines 599, 812 to 815). No source for an external market was found.
- **RFP-013 and RFP-020.** RFP-013 lists RFP-020 as the source of "the external price feed required for market price and redemption rate computation" (front matter lines 6 to 7); RFP-019 says the market price input can come only from the LEZ DEX (lines 78 to 85). Not reconciled in the texts.
- **RFP-022 caps.** They are rate limits per rolling window, enforced per account (Functionality #11); no cap on total wrapped supply is specified.
- **Wrapped BTC route.** RFP-022 puts wrapped Bitcoin out of scope (lines 103 to 107) but names WBTC as a decimals example (line 319); whether WBTC bridged from Ethereum through RFP-022 is a second wrapped BTC route alongside Strata is not addressed in the texts.
- **Strata trust model.** Alpen's docs say deposits go to an N-of-N multisig and one functional operator guarantees safety and liveness; the 2024 blog post spoke of 1-of-N liveness for withdrawals; press coverage says one honest operator keeps the bridge secure. The internal estimate says combined trust is the weaker of Strata's 1-of-N and LEZ's sequencer threshold.
- **Strata mainnet.** Planned for 2025 (January 2025); not live on 2026-11-19; no date published.
- **Logos mainnet date.** "Early 2027" was seen only in a search snippet of a staging page now returning 404; not verified.
- **Figures in sections 2 and 3.** Conflicts are recorded in the rows: Aave Harmony bad debt ($1.24M against $751k), Nomad loss ($190M against $156M), Multichain loss ($126M to $210M), Venus liquidation of the BNB Bridge attacker ($30M against $63M), KelpDAO bad debt on Aave (several estimates, one source says zero), Mezo minimum collateral ratio (150% against 110%), Felix UBTC LTV (50% against 40%), Eisenberg bad debt ($1.6M against $1.78M), Egorov bad debt ($10M against $11.5M).
- **Gaps.** RFP-022 award status; Strata third-party account path; Alpen mainnet bridge parameters; current Gyroscope bounds; Sky Atlas concentration limits; Hubble's wrapped ETH and BTC at launch; launch caps for Mezo, Felix and Arkadiko.
